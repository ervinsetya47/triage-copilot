## Finding summary (for the record)

OptimismPortal2.finalizeWithdrawalTransactionExternalProof: an unprivileged attacker can finalize another user's direct-portal withdrawal before the target contract's unlock, burning 1 ether permanently (finalizedWithdrawals[hash] set to true, no replay possible). The attacker receives nothing; the victim's funds are destroyed. This is publicly disclosed before the competition: Sherlock Optimism 2023-01 H-1 and 2024-02 issue #155 describe the same mechanism (loss of funds), and Base's own OptimismPortal2 NatSpec states the limitation: 'Messages sent directly to the OptimismPortal have no form of replayability'. It is unmodified upstream OP logic.

**PoC notes:** PoC passed 3/3 on the local Everest devnet: attacker finalizes the victim's direct-portal withdrawal, 1 ether is burned permanently, and the victim cannot replay the withdrawal.
